Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ADHD in Progress is made by Dane Bonnigson. It's built to collect nothing about you.

What stays on your device

Your settings (handles, caption sign-off, hashtags, optional Unsplash key), any edits you make to pages, and the list of pages you've posted are stored only on your device. There are no servers and no accounts, and we can't see any of it.

Photos and videos

When you pick a photo or video for a background, the app reads it on your device to make the image. When you save a post, the app adds the image or video to your photo library. It doesn't read the rest of your library.

Unsplash (optional)

If you search Unsplash, your search words are sent to Unsplash to return photos, and the photo you choose is downloaded from Unsplash. Unsplash's privacy policy applies to those requests. We don't store your searches.

Image Playground (optional)

Images are created on your device by Apple Intelligence under Apple's privacy terms.

Sharing

Images, videos and captions go only where you send them through the share sheet.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13.
