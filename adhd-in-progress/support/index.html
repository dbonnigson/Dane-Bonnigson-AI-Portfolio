Common questions

Can I change a page's text? Yes. Open a page and tap Edit. Your changes stay on your device, and you can restore the original text from the same screen.

Why didn't the app post for me? Social apps don't let other apps post on your behalf. Tap Post, choose the app in the share sheet, then paste the caption, which is already copied.

Where are my saved images and videos? In your Photos library, when you tap Save. The app only asks to add photos, not to read your library.

Unsplash search says it needs a key. Photo search is optional. Every other background option works without it.

Is this medical advice? No. ADHD in Progress is for education and encouragement and doesn't replace care from a qualified professional.
