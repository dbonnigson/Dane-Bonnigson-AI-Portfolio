Your First Prompt

Paste this to try CRAFT

A safe, no-private-data starter you can run immediately:

You are a friendly internal-communications writer. We're moving our weekly team meeting from Monday to Wednesday at 10am, starting next week. Write a short note (under 70 words) to announce it, warm and clear, ending with one line asking people to update their calendars.

Close but not perfect? Reply in the same chat: "make it a touch shorter and add a thank-you line." Don't start over.

Remember

The one safety rule

If you wouldn't post it on a public bulletin board, don't paste it in. Use placeholders, strip private details, and keep sensitive work on the approved tool. See the What's Safe to Paste checklist.
