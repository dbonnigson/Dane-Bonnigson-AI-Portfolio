About

From the classroom to AI-powered teams.

I train teams on AI and build the AI tools they use. Through PalmettoGroup.AI, the AI training and consulting division of ZenFrog LLC, I've built exam-prep training and ongoing content for clients, and shipped an AI agent that sends uncertain cases to a person, an AI video editor in use through 20+ releases, and 3 iPhone apps.
