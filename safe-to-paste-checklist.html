"What's Safe to Paste" Checklist

One rule, one do-not-paste list, three habits. Print it and keep it by your screen.

The One-Line Test

Treat a public AI tool like a public postcard

Free and personal chatbots may use what you type, and you don't control where it goes. So before you paste, run one test:

If you wouldn't post it on a public bulletin board, don't paste it in.

Never paste these into a public / personal tool

  • Passwords and login codes
  • Full Social Security or financial account numbers
  • Other people's personal details — names, contact info, health information
  • Confidential company, client, or patient information
Three Habits

Stay safe without slowing down

  • Swap in placeholders. "A patient," "Company X," "my coworker" instead of real names and numbers — the AI doesn't need the real ones to help.
  • Strip before you paste. Rewriting an email? Drop the signature, phone numbers, and account IDs first — paste only the part you need rewritten.
  • Use the approved tool for sensitive work. A provided business AI account has different data protections than a personal login.
